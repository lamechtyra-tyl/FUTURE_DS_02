Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
An end-to-end analysis of customer churn for a SaaS subscription business, using Excel for data preparation and analysis and Power BI for an interactive dashboard.

Project Overview
The goal of this project is to answer three questions:
How many customers are we losing, and how fast?
Where is churn concentrated (by plan, segment, region and cohort)?
How much recurring revenue does churn cost the business?

Churn rate is fairly even across plans (about 18-21%), segments (about 18-20%) and regions, so no single group stands out as a churn problem.
Revenue loss is not even. Enterprise (~$150K) and Scale (~$107K) plans account for roughly 75% of churned MRR, because they carry higher monthly prices.
By segment, mid-market (~$137K) and enterprise (~$123K) customers account for the largest share of lost revenue.
Recent cohorts show lower churn, but this is partly because newer customers have not had time to cancel yet. Cohorts should be compared at the same age before drawing conclusions.
Recommendations
Prioritise retention efforts (customer success outreach, renewal incentives, early-warning monitoring) on Enterprise and Scale accounts, where each cancellation costs the most.
Compare cohorts at equal age (e.g. churn within the first 6 months) for a fairer view of whether retention is improving.
